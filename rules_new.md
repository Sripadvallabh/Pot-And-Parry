# Pot & Parry

Standard snooker rules apply except for the changes below.

## Setup

Place the six colors on their usual spots and one red midway between the blue and pink spots. Respot every potted ball; the red returns to its starting position.

## Players

Play with two players or four players. Four may play individually or as two teams of two.

A **side** means one individual or one team. A team shares one score.

## Basic play

Black is the ball on for the opening stroke. Thereafter, any object ball may be nominated as the ball on. If unclear, the striker identifies it when asked.

Normal turns rotate by side. On a team side, teammates alternate its normal turns in a fixed order.

A **round** contains one normal turn for each side.

## Claims

Red cannot be claimed. Each color is either **open** with no owner or **claimed** by one side. All six colors start open. Mark each claim; a team owns its claims jointly.

Claim limits are:

- three colors per side in a two-sided game; or
- two colors per player in a four-player individual game.

Potting an open color scores its value and claims it unless the side is already at its limit, in which case it remains open.

A claimed color scores only for its owner. Other sides score zero for potting it. A claimed yellow or green scores four for its owner; other claimed colors retain their values.

Claims remain after the balls are respotted.

## Winning

The target is exactly 50. Reaching it ends the side's current play and creates a provisional finish until the round ends.

- No further parries may be called that round. Unfinished sides take only their remaining normal turns.
- A provisionally finished side skips any remaining normal turn. Reaching the target does not release its claims.
- At the end of the round, one side on its target wins; multiple sides on their targets use the blue-ball shoot-out.

A legal pot that would exceed the target scores zero but continues the turn and may still create a claim. Potting another side's claimed color also scores zero and continues the turn.

### Blue-ball shoot-out

Each tied side takes a direct blue attempt from the D into a declared pocket. The blue may touch only the jaws of that pocket. After equal attempts, eliminate sides below the leading shoot-out score and repeat until one remains. Teammates alternate attempts.

## Parry

A side may call one parry per normal turn before any stroke, including after a successful pot.

The challenger names another side's claimed color as the **challenged color**, then nominates any object ball for the **setup stroke**. Red and the challenged color are both valid setup balls.

The challenger must play a legal setup stroke without a pot. A foul or pot cancels the parry, applies the penalty and ends the challenger's turn.

After a legal setup, the owner plays the challenged color as the ball on in an extra visit. For a team, the teammate next due plays without advancing the teammate order.

The challenged player must genuinely attempt to hit the challenged color. Clearly choosing another shot instead concedes the game. A genuine failed attempt is a parry foul.

- **Legal stroke without a pot:** The parry is defended and the color stays claimed.
- **Legal pot:** The parry is defended; the owner scores and may continue the extra visit. Any later foul is ordinary.
- **Foul:** The owner pays the penalty, the color becomes open, and the challenger resumes the interrupted turn from the resulting position.

After a defended parry, normal turn order resumes. The extra visit is not a normal turn and cannot contain another parry. The challenger cannot call another after resuming the interrupted turn.

## Timed showdown

With a visible clock, complete the current round when 30 minutes expires. If nobody ends the round on 50, each side records its score and declares a fixed personal target: the next higher multiple of five or the next higher multiple of ten.

Each side also gets a loss line ten points below its recorded score. For example, a side on 33 may choose 35 or 40 and has a loss line of 23.

During the showdown, scores may fall below zero. Reaching or crossing the loss line eliminates that side.

Start a new round from the existing position and continue in normal order. Each personal target replaces 50 for the finish procedure under [Winning](#winning). Multiple finishers tie; the sole remaining side after eliminations wins. Foul penalties change neither targets nor loss lines.

## Free ball

After a foul, a free ball is available only if the incoming side is snookered on red, every open color and every color it owns. Eligibility ignores the exact target.

The side may nominate another side's claimed color to represent any eligible ball. It scores that ball's value without changing claims. Other free-ball rules apply normally.

## Fouls

Foul points are deducted from the offending side. Scores stop at zero outside the timed showdown. The foul-and-a-miss rule is not used.
